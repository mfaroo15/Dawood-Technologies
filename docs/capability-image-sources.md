## Generation prompts

Each prompt combines the following common specification with its subject paragraph below:

> Create one original landscape editorial website capability image, 3:2 ratio, no collage, no border, no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermark. Premium corporate technology photography/render hybrid, realistic materials, cool blue-gray and white palette with restrained azure highlights, medium brightness, soft architectural daylight, rich detail but clean centered framing, no purple, no neon, no robots, no brains. This image is one of four coordinated images for Dawood Technologies; use a cohesive polished enterprise aesthetic.

**Services**

> Subject: an immaculate enterprise infrastructure environment: close perspective down a short row of modern server racks with silver-blue surfaces, glass reflections, neat cable architecture, softly glowing blue status lights, no visible readable labels. Architectural realism and professional atmosphere.

**AI Nexus**

> Subject: a sophisticated original digital intelligence network: a small luminous blue processor at the center of a layered glass-and-silver computing surface, delicate connected nodes and circuit paths extending across the image in three-dimensional space, subtle restrained blue illumination. No brain, humanoid, text or giant AI letters. Elegant and technically grounded.

**Solutions**

> Subject: a modern business software and systems architecture environment: a clean architectural desk-scale arrangement of translucent glass interface planes and modular blue-gray system blocks connected logically, abstract interface charts without any text or characters, realistic glass, soft daylight, thoughtful business technology architecture, no people or logos.

**Industries**

> Subject: a modern commercial operating environment: wide architectural view of a contemporary logistics and manufacturing campus with a clean warehouse, structured loading bays, glass office frontage and landscaped access roads, realistic industrial materials, white and silver-blue buildings under soft clear daylight. No branding, signage, vehicles with logos or people. Enterprise operations and infrastructure.

No approved mockup image was present in the supplied attachment. Layout follows the written card proportions and section requirements; an exact reference-image comparison remains unavailable.
